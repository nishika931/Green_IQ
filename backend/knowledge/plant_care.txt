## ROSE PLANT CARE

### Soil and Drainage
Roses grow best in fertile, well-draining soil. Good drainage allows excess water to escape and helps prevent waterlogged soil. Soil that remains wet for long periods can contribute to root problems.

### Sunlight
Roses generally benefit from several hours of direct sunlight each day. Their sunlight needs can vary depending on the rose variety, climate, and growing conditions.

### Watering
Rose watering needs depend on soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall, sunlight, and the plant's growing environment. Check soil moisture before watering instead of relying only on a fixed schedule. Avoid keeping the soil constantly waterlogged.

### Yellow Leaves
Yellow leaves on roses can be associated with overwatering, poor drainage, nutrient deficiencies, pests, diseases, environmental stress, or natural aging. Examine the pattern of yellowing and the condition of the soil before deciding on a likely cause.

### Pruning and Air Circulation
Regular pruning and adequate air circulation help maintain healthy rose growth. Remove dead or damaged growth when appropriate, and avoid overcrowding plants.

### Common Rose Care Questions
- Why are my rose leaves turning yellow? Possible causes include watering problems, poor drainage, nutrient deficiencies, pests, diseases, and environmental stress.
- How often should I water my rose? The frequency depends on soil moisture and environmental conditions. Check the soil before watering.

- Does Green IQ recommend a particular fertilizer for roses? The current Green IQ knowledge does not identify a specific fertilizer or dosage.


## TOMATO PLANT CARE

### Soil and Drainage
Tomato plants need soil that supports healthy root growth and drains excess water. Waterlogged soil can damage roots and contribute to plant stress.

### Sunlight
Tomato plants generally need plenty of sunlight for healthy growth. Their requirements can vary with the variety, climate, and growing conditions.

### Watering
Tomato plants benefit from consistent watering appropriate to their growing conditions. Avoid excessive watering and waterlogged soil. Consider soil moisture, temperature, rainfall, and the plant's stage of growth when deciding when to water.

### Yellow Leaves
Yellow leaves may be associated with overwatering, nutrient deficiencies, pests, diseases, or environmental stress. Check soil moisture and inspect the plant for visible symptoms before deciding on a cause.

### Air Circulation
Good air circulation can help reduce conditions that favor some fungal diseases. Avoid unnecessarily overcrowding tomato plants.

### Common Tomato Care Questions
- Why are my tomato leaves yellow? Possible causes include watering problems, nutrient deficiencies, pests, diseases, and environmental stress.
- Should I water my tomato plant every day? The current Green IQ knowledge does not prescribe a universal daily watering schedule.
- Does Green IQ specify an exact fertilizer dosage for tomatoes? No specific fertilizer dosage is provided in the current knowledge.


## MONEY PLANT CARE

### Light
Money plants generally prefer bright, indirect light and may tolerate lower-light conditions. Growth can vary depending on the amount and quality of available light.

### Watering
Allow the soil to partially dry between watering. Check the soil before adding more water, and avoid keeping it constantly wet.

### Yellow Leaves
Yellow leaves may be associated with overwatering, poor drainage, insufficient light, natural aging, or other environmental stress. Check whether the soil remains wet and inspect the plant's growing conditions.

### Root Problems
Excessive watering and poor drainage can contribute to root problems. If the soil stays wet for long periods, review the pot's drainage and watering practices.

### Common Money Plant Care Questions
- How should I water my money plant? Allow the soil to partially dry between watering and avoid excessive moisture.
- Why are my money plant leaves yellow? Possible causes include overwatering, poor drainage, insufficient light, and natural aging.
- Can a money plant tolerate low light? It can tolerate lower-light conditions, although its growth may vary with available light.
