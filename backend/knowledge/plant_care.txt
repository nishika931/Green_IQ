ROSE PLANT CARE

Roses need well-draining soil and generally prefer several hours
of sunlight each day.

Watering should depend on soil moisture, temperature, humidity,
rainfall, and the plant's environment.

Avoid keeping the soil constantly waterlogged.

Yellow leaves can be caused by overwatering, poor drainage,
insufficient nutrients, pests, or environmental stress.

Roses benefit from regular pruning and adequate air circulation.


TOMATO PLANT CARE

Tomato plants generally need plenty of sunlight and consistent
watering.

Avoid excessive watering because waterlogged soil can damage roots.

Yellow leaves may be caused by overwatering, nutrient deficiencies,
pests, diseases, or environmental stress.

Good air circulation can help reduce some fungal disease problems.


MONEY PLANT CARE

Money plants generally prefer bright indirect light but can tolerate
lower-light conditions.

Allow the soil to partially dry between watering.

Excessive watering can cause root problems and yellow leaves.

Yellow leaves may be associated with overwatering, poor drainage,
insufficient light, or natural aging.


SNAKE PLANT CARE

Snake plants are generally tolerant of lower-light conditions.

They should not be kept constantly waterlogged.

Allow the soil to partially dry between watering.

Overwatering can contribute to root problems.


ALOE VERA CARE

Aloe vera generally prefers bright light.

Avoid keeping the soil continuously wet.

Allow the soil to dry between watering.

Overwatering can contribute to root problems.


GENERAL PLANT CARE

Plants need appropriate light, water, soil, nutrients, temperature,
and air circulation for healthy growth.

Overwatering is a common plant-care problem.

Yellow leaves can have many possible causes, including watering
problems, insufficient or excessive light, nutrient deficiencies,
pests, diseases, environmental stress, or natural aging.

Before diagnosing a plant problem, consider the plant species,
watering schedule, soil condition, light exposure, temperature,
humidity, and visible symptoms.


PLANT PESTS

Common plant pests include aphids, spider mites, mealybugs,
whiteflies, and scale insects.

Pests may cause yellowing, curling, spots, holes, sticky residue,
or distorted leaves.

When checking for pests, inspect both the upper and lower surfaces
of leaves and examine new growth carefully.
